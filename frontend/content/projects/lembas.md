# Overview

Lembas is a calm academic workspace that gives students one dependable place to see what matters today and why it matters.

## Context

The concept was shaped around the weekly rhythm of a student: switch between subjects, collect references, and recover from a missed day without losing the thread.

## Product decisions

Rather than turn study into a dashboard of numbers, the experience uses clear next actions and a visible context trail. Subjects become spaces with notes, links, and open questions.

## What I built

I designed the information architecture, built the responsive React interface, and defined a small set of primitives for progress, deadlines, and resource capture. The prototype was tested with keyboard navigation from the first iteration.

## Lessons learned

Calm interfaces still need a strong point of view. Removing noise only helped after the product made a clear decision about what deserves to be visible now.
