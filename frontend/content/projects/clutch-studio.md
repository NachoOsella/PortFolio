# Overview

Clutch Studio needed a website that could feel expressive without becoming a catalogue of effects.

## Direction

The work is given room to breathe: large stills, short notes from the team, and a booking path that feels like an invitation instead of a form.

## Interaction

Motion is used to preserve spatial context as projects open and close. On reduced motion settings, the same hierarchy remains intact with instant transitions.

## Lessons learned

A visual system earns personality through pacing as much as color. The quiet moments are what make the strong moments legible.
