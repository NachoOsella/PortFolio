# Overview

El Brasero makes a familiar weekly ritual feel effortless: browse the menu, choose a pickup time, and get back to the evening.

## The brief

The old flow made people jump between menus, phone calls, and screenshots. The new website treats the menu as the primary interface and makes decisions legible at a glance.

## Details

The work focused on quick loading, touch targets, clear modifiers, and a checkout sequence that never loses the order summary. Content is written so the restaurant team can update prices and availability without developer support.

## Lessons learned

Commerce is mostly about reducing uncertainty. A good product answer often looks like a small line of copy in the exact moment someone needs it.
