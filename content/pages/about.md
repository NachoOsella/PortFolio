## About me

I am Ignacio Osella, a full-stack developer from Córdoba, Argentina. I like the seam between product intent and the systems that make it dependable.

## How I work

I start by making the problem legible. Then I shape a small interface, keep the domain boundary explicit, and leave the next person a codebase they can understand.

My day-to-day tools are React, TypeScript, Java, Spring Boot, relational databases, Docker, and a healthy amount of written notes.

## Currently learning

I am spending time on resilient frontend architecture, better accessibility defaults, and the small operational details that make content systems trustworthy.

## Outside the editor

I keep a running list of interfaces that explain complex things well. Most of my best ideas start as a note about why one screen felt easier to use than another.
